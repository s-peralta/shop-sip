Giveaway terms & conditions

How it works. The brand you enter when you request your spot goes into the draw. Entries close 11:59pm ET on Friday 9 October 2026. Twenty-five brands are drawn at random. The $200 credit belongs to the brand, is spent in person at Mejuri Ossington on 14 October, and cannot be transferred or taken as cash.

No purchase necessary. Open to residents of Ontario aged 19 or over who register and attend the Shop & Sip event at Mejuri Ossington on 14 October 2026. Entries close at 11:59pm ET on Friday 9 October 2026. One entry per person; one credit per brand. Brands entered must be independent and Toronto-based.

Twenty-five (25) prizes are available, each a $200 CAD in-store credit. Twenty-five brands are selected at random from valid entries received before the entry deadline. The prize is awarded to the brand named on the entry and may be redeemed only by a representative of that brand attending the event. Odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received. The named recipient must correctly answer a mathematical skill-testing question before being declared a winner, must attend the event, and is notified by email before it. Credits are redeemable in person at Mejuri Ossington on the night of 14 October 2026 only and are applied at checkout. They carry no cash value, cannot be carried over, exchanged, transferred or redeemed online, and are forfeited if unclaimed on the night.

Entry constitutes consent to be photographed or filmed at the event and for that material to be used by Nstruct and its event partners for promotional purposes. Nstruct reserves the right to verify eligibility, disqualify entries, and amend, suspend or withdraw this promotion at any time without notice.

This promotion is administered solely by Nstruct. Mejuri, Shopify, Domaine and Channable are event partners and are not sponsors, administrators or otherwise responsible for the promotion. Void where prohibited by law. For full rules or any question about this promotion, email info@nstruct.io.
